## Content standards

This portfolio contains only what is supported by evidence or confirmed directly:

- No education or academic history.
- No invented achievements, statistics, clients, or testimonials.
- Internships are described as internships, with their real scope and status.
- The social-engineering lab is published as concepts, methodology, and defensive outcomes
  only — no payloads or executable detail.
- No contact form that fakes a success message; contact routes to a real inbox.
- Labs are not listed until they are completed.
